Channel Dashboard

Channel Dashboard is a personal, single-user tool. Its owner uses it to see the numbers for his own YouTube channels in one place on his own computer.

What it does

  • Reads analytics for the owner's own YouTube channels (views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, audience retention and thumbnail click-rate) through the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs.
  • Reads public video and channel information (titles, publish dates, public view counts) through the YouTube Data API, including for a short list of public competitor channels.
  • Shows the results as a local web page on the owner's computer.

What it does not do

  • It is read-only. It never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything on YouTube.
  • It is not offered to the public. Only the owner's own channels are connected.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data.

See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
