Privacy policy: Channel Dashboard

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Channel Dashboard is a personal tool used by one person, its owner, to view the analytics of his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it accesses and what happens to that data.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects one of his channels, he signs in with Google and grants two read-only permissions:

  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): channel and video details such as titles, publish dates and public view counts.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): views, watch time, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources, audience retention and thumbnail impressions and click-rate. The app also asks the YouTube Reporting API to prepare the thumbnail report.

It also reads public information about a few public competitor channels through the YouTube Data API. It does not access email, contacts, payment details or any Google data other than the above.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner a dashboard of his own channels' performance. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

All data, and the sign-in keys Google issues, are stored only on the owner's own computer. There is no server and no online database. The owner may use an AI assistant on that computer to read and summarise the stored figures for his own analysis.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept on the owner's computer until he deletes it. Deleting the tool's data folder removes it.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revoking, the app can no longer read new data.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
